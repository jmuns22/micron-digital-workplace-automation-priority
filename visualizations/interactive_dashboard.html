Directional Estimate: Scaling to Micron's Actual Workforce

The model was built at a 5,000-employee reference scale, the size most public automation benchmarks are reported at. Scaled proportionally to Micron's ~53,000-employee FY2025 headcount (per its 10-K), the combined savings identified would extrapolate to roughly $4.1M/year. This is directional, not a budget figure: see the README's Limitations section.
What if the workforce covered isn't 53,000? 53,000
Baseline: 53,000 (Micron's total FY2025 headcount, per its 10-K). Try a smaller number, e.g. one site or one function, not the whole company, to see how the estimate scales down.
